01 / Areas of interest

Building a bridge between
drawings and data.

Research interests centered on bringing spatial understanding and structured automation to architectural workflows.

R / 01

Drawing understanding

Investigating extraction of walls, doors, windows, dimensions, room boundaries, and relationships from PDF and DWG drawings.

R / 02

Semantic BIM generation

Exploring how recognized geometry becomes editable, meaningful building elements rather than an unstructured 3D mesh.

R / 03

Agentic design automation

Studying AI-guided design sequences, constraint checking, and software agents that can assist with repetitive BIM modeling tasks.
